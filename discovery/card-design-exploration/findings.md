# Card design exploration: findings

**Kind of hop: an exploration** of how the pairing card could look, written as a design space. It chooses nothing; the redesign that follows does. Charter answers, strongest first:

1. **Layouts (answered).** Four layouts of the same card are mocked up on the six pairings already built, each on a phone-sized and a computer-sized screen, as a list and as one card: A three zones (the maintainer's layout), B words first, C matched pair with a money ledger, D glance row that opens. On a computer, A and C put all three things in view at once. On a phone the order decides: A puts the first dollar amount about 90 characters in and the words about 1,400 (card 4), B puts the words about 90 in and the money about 1,040, C the words about 120 in and the money about 1,230; D's list card is about 500 to 600 characters against 2,500 to 3,700 for the others. See `design-space.md#layouts-side-by-side`.
2. **Doubt, highlights and the 'reading, not a finding' note (answered).** Three independent choices: where the strip sits (under the zones, between words and money, or as a top label), how doubt is shown (badge alone, with its reason, or opening) and how shared words are marked (highlight in both texts, chips, or a plain 'nothing shared' line; two of five built cards share nothing of substance). The strip is the last block of A to C and is hidden in D's list rows, which is the main risk found: a reader who stops early never meets it.
3. **Money through several committees and outside spending (answered, partly by mockup).** One block per committee labelled 'this committee only' avoids the shape of a sum; a ledger table is compact but its column of amounts has that shape. Outside spending sits in its own dashed box with a label and a note, and no card builds a total of it with anything. Of the five built cards none has outside spending and none more than two committees, so card 6 (Mahmood and GrowSF, outside spending only, three filer lines) is the stress case; the mockups build refuses any page whose text holds a sum of committee totals.
4. **Scanning versus reading versus checking (partly).** Scanners need names, shared words, one line per committee, the badge and a flag for outside spending; a closer reader needs the priority, the strip and the full money lines; a checker needs the row-level filing table, links at the claim and the retrieval date. This comes from the content and the mockups; no reader was timed.

**Not found:** any test with readers; any view of the mockups in a browser (their phone behaviour is unverified). Open questions are at the end of `design-space.md`.

## Files

- `design-space.md` and `design-space.json`: 4 problems, 11 dimensions, 34 options with sketches and trade-offs; the fixed content of every card; the side-by-side comparison; open questions.
- `mockups/index.html`: the four layouts in phone and computer frames. `mockups/option-<a-d>-list.html` and `mockups/option-<a-d>-card.html`: each layout as a list of six cards and as one card on its own page. `mockups/reading-order.json`: text-order counts used above.
- `card_design_lib.py`, `card_layouts.py`, `build_mockups.py`: write the mockups from `data/` (`python build_mockups.py`); `tests/test_mockups.py` checks the fixed rules. `data/pairing-cards.json` is a copy of the Official funding map's cards; `data/outside-spending-example.json` is the worked pairing used as card 6.
