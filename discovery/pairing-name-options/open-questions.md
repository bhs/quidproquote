# Open questions

What was looked for and not found, what is worth asking and of whom, and which claims are not verified.

## Looked for and not found

- **Any shared name for the three-part unit.** Digital Democracy, the SF Ethics Commission's dashboards, three San Francisco newsrooms, MapLight and ProPublica were read (`comparable-sites.md`); none gives one noun to words, priority and money together.
- **Evidence on how readers take "pairing", "alignment", "overlap", "side by side" or "connection".** No study or reader test was found, and none has been run. `reader-check.md` is the instrument for a small check.
- **Reporters' style-guide rules** for wording a contribution next to a quote. None found.
- **OpenSecrets, Voter's Edge, MapLight's own site, Follow the Money, Sunlight's Influence Explorer and LittleSis pages** were not read. Their naming is not described here except where marked "(recalled)".
- **The full text of the Robertson et al. experiment** could not be opened; only the abstract-level snippet was read.

## What would be worth asking, and of whom

For the maintainer:

1. **"Pay-to-play" in the second Objective.** The Objective says "let a reporter quickly understand pay-to-play speech". The Vision's first non-goal rules out implying that donations cause what politicians say, and `comparable-sites.md` describes "pay to play" in law as an exchange. Was the phrase meant as shorthand for the maintainer's own use, or as a word the site might show? The Objective stands as written; this hop only reports the disagreement.
2. **Who the name is for.** The Vision leaves the core users beyond "the general public" open. A reporter can be given a word like "lead" or "Schedule A"; a resident first meeting the site cannot. Which should the unit's name serve first?
3. **The donor side when the source is a news report.** Project Memory: "fine to read reports from credible news organizations that suggest priorities for large donors or PACs as long as we track sources." A heading that says "stated priority" or "in its own words" is accurate only for the donor's own page. What should the heading say when the words are an outlet's report of a priority (for example "reported by {outlet}")?
4. **Which people to ask.** `reader-check.md` needs three to six people, at least one reporter or watchdog. The maintainer names them.
5. **Whether a rename of the public path `/pairings/` is acceptable** if the unit's name changes (it needs a redirect, `where-the-name-appears.md` item 13).

For later work, from the evidence: whether reporters read the strip's guard sentence before or after the money (not knowable from the repository); how many of the site's visitors arrive on a permalink and so never see the lede that defines the unit.
