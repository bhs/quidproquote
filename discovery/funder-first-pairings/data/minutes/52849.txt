Members Present: Connie Chan, Chyanne Chen, Matt Dorsey, Jackie Fielder, Bilal Mahmood,
Rafael Mandelman, Myrna Melgar, Danny Sauter, Stephen Sherrill,
Shamann Walton, and Alan Wong

The Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco met in regular session on
Tuesday, July 14, 2026, with President Rafael Mandelman presiding.

SPECIAL ORDER 2:00 P.M. - Mayor's Appearance Before The Board

Mayor Daniel Lurie addressed the Board of Supervisors regarding the reduction of crime, homeless
encampments, and overdoses; the increase of public transit riders, issuance of permits, and
business investments; the strengthening of the City's safety net and the successful collaboration on
the budget; organized community events that have served certain neighborhoods; and his continued
desire to partner with the Members of the Board to ensure a safer and cleaner City where everyone
has the opportunity to thrive. President Mandelman then invited Supervisor Fielder to ask her
question related to the noticed topic.

Recommendation of the Rules Committee

[Charter Amendment - Housing Trust Fund]260537
Sponsors: Melgar; Walton, Sauter, Sherrill, Dorsey, Wong, Mahmood, Mandelman, Fielder
and Chen
Charter Amendment (Second Draft) to amend the Charter of the City and County of San Francisco to
increase the amount of funding the City must appropriate to the Housing Trust Fund each fiscal year
that is used for the creation, acquisition, and rehabilitation of affordable rental and ownership housing;
at an election to be held on November 3, 2026.

Supervisor Chen requested to be added as a co-sponsor.

ORDERED SUBMITTED by the following vote:

Ayes: 11 - Chan, Chen, Dorsey, Fielder, Mahmood, Mandelman, Melgar, Sauter, Sherrill, Walton,
Wong

Recommendation of the Government Audit and Oversight Committee

[Planning, Administrative, Building Codes - Inclusionary Housing Program Updates and Reductions; Development Impact Fee Reductions]

260538

Sponsors: Mayor; Melgar, Dorsey, Sherrill and Sauter
Ordinance amending the Planning Code to: 1) reduce Inclusionary Affordable Housing Program
requirements for projects of 25 units or more; 2) delete Inclusionary Affordable Housing Program
requirements for projects under 25 units; 5) delete certain Article 4 affordable housing and development impact fees.

Supervisor Fielder, seconded by Supervisor Melgar, moved that this Ordinance be AMENDED, AN
AMENDMENT OF THE WHOLE BEARING SAME TITLE, on Page 53, Line 9, by changing 'For' to 'Except as
provided in subsection (a)(2), for'; (2) In the Mission Area Plan, for any Ownership Housing Project, the number of
Affordable Units constructed on-site shall be 8% of all units constructed on the project site. The motion carried by the following vote:

Ayes: 6 - Chan, Chen, Fielder, Mahmood, Melgar, Walton

Noes: 5 - Dorsey, Mandelman, Sauter, Sherrill, Wong

PASSED ON FIRST READING AS AMENDED by the following vote:
Ayes: 9 - Chan, Dorsey, Fielder, Mahmood, Mandelman, Melgar, Sauter, Sherrill, Wong

Noes: 2 - Chen, Walton

Recommendations of the Public Safety and Neighborhood Services Committee

[Administrative Code - Expanding Drug-Free Permanent Supportive Housing]251003
Sponsors: Dorsey; Mandelman, Sherrill, Sauter, Mahmood and Wong
Ordinance amending the Administrative Code to state that it is City policy to expand the availability of
Site-Based Permanent Supportive Housing that prohibits on-site illicit drug use among residents.

Supervisor Dorsey, seconded by Supervisor Sherrill, moved that this Ordinance be AMENDED, AN
AMENDMENT OF THE WHOLE BEARING SAME TITLE, on Page 8, Line 23, by adding 'evictions and'. The
motion carried by the following vote:

Ayes: 10 - Chan, Chen, Dorsey, Fielder, Mahmood, Mandelman, Melgar, Sauter, Sherrill, Wong

Noes: 1 - Walton
