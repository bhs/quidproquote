# Official funding map: which donors with stated priorities gave through committees tied to the Mayor and the eleven supervisors, and where each worked pairing now stands

**Kind of hop: a survey** of sources outside the project (the Ethics Commission's campaign finance Transactions and Filers datasets on DataSF, the Commission's published contribution rules, donors' own pages, and the city's caption transcripts), written as **findings**. It makes no decisions. It starts from the money, which the earlier worked pairings (`../worked-pairing-examples/findings.md`) did not: they started from matched words and found money for one of six pairs, and that one was outside spending, not a gift.

The map itself is `funding-map.md` (and `funding-map.json`), the five pairings are `pairings.md` (and `pairings.json`); both are written by `python build_map.py` from the snapshot in `data/`, which `python collect.py` refreshes (network needed). This file says what they show and how far to trust it. Money was retrieved 2026-10-30, data loaded through 2026-10-30 (`data/money_snapshot.json`).

## Summary by charter question

1. **Which committees are tied to each official?** 55 committees for the twelve officials (Mayor Lurie and Supervisors Chan, Sherrill, Sauter, Wong, Mahmood, Dorsey, Melgar, Mandelman, Fielder, Walton and Chen), listed one table per official in `funding-map.md` section 1 with relation, status, Schedule A rows and total, 24-hour-only total and last filing. Only the **Mayor** has committees "for a ballot measure" carrying his name: three (`Mayor Lurie's Yes on D, E & F Committee`, `Mayor Lurie's Yes on H Committee`, `Daniel Lurie for Prop E`, the last terminated), holding $18.6M of Schedule A between them. **No supervisor has a ballot-measure committee carrying their name** in either dataset (a search of every committee name for each surname, and of all active measure committees, found none). The tie for a measure committee is its name; the data has no field for who controls a committee (see Open questions). Supervisors' committees are mostly their own candidate and officeholder committees for supervisor, plus committees for other offices (Democratic County Central Committee, College Board, School Board), and a few primarily formed committees supporting or opposing them, which are not the official's own and are shown apart.
2. **Who gave, and how is an organisation's own committee recognised?** Section 2 lists the largest organisation and individual givers per official, section 3 the recognition. A committee giver row carries an FPPC id in 562 of 568 cases; 338 of those ids resolve to a committee registered with the Ethics Commission, whose registered name states the organisation or its sponsor. That resolves the question the earlier hop left open: the bare name "SF Forward" (and "SF Chamber of Commerce 21st Century Committee") carries the same FPPC id (891575) as "San Francisco Forward sponsored by San Francisco Chamber of Commerce", so it is the Chamber's committee. The rule used is: the FPPC id first, then the name's words, then the sponsor words in a name; anything else is left unmatched and shown as filed. 17 donor families were defined, taking 135 committee giver rows (19 by id, 116 by name). Where a committee's sponsor is "Labor Organizations", no single organisation is named and none is assigned.
3. **Which of those donors publish a priority at a citable link?** Of the 17 families, 8 have a statement quoted from their own page, verified on the page today and carried with link and retrieval date: the Chamber, Airbnb, the San Francisco Police Officers Association (POA), SEIU Local 1021, Govern For California, the San Francisco Apartment Association, BOMA San Francisco and GrowSF. Nine have none found (section 4 of `funding-map.md`): SEIU 2015, IFPTE Local 21, the Labor Council, the Realtors, TWU Local 250-A, Operating Engineers Local 3, Laborers Local 261, Emerson Collective and Neighbors for a Better San Francisco. "None found" means none on pages a plain request returns; see Open questions for what was not readable. Individuals: of the large individual givers to the Mayor's measure committees, one (Jeremy Stoppelman) has a first-party statement in the earlier survey's class A; Moritz and Chakrabarti are class B, most others C or D.
4. **Do the city's limits mean organisations' money reaches officials mostly as outside spending or through ballot measure committees?** By dollars of gifts, **yes for ballot-measure committees, and for committees for other offices; no single answer for outside spending, which is a separate bucket.** The rules: $500 per source per election to a candidate committee, no corporate, LLC or LLP gifts to candidate committees, no limit on non-candidate committees, candidate-controlled measure committees or independent spending; county central committee candidate committees are also outside the limit (Ethics Commission sources in `funding-map.md` section 6). In the filings, organisations and committees gave $207K of $15.3M (1.4%) to the officials' own supervisor and mayor committees, against $5.59M of $18.6M (30.0%) to the ballot-measure committees carrying the Mayor's name and $220K of $983K (22.4%) to their committees for another office. Of the $6.33M that organisations gave across all committee classes, 88% went to the Mayor's measure committees. Outside spending naming the twelve officials (form 496) is $11.06M over all spenders, of which $1.14M is by committees a donor family's names take; it is not added to any gift.

**How each came by.** Each began with a family row in the snapshot (a donor family's gift to a committee tied to the official), then a search of the caption transcripts for the official's words on a subject the donor's own page speaks to. Pairings 1 and 2 use the Mayor's opening remarks at his appearance before the Board (the transcript names him before and after: speaker credit low doubt). Pairing 3 uses a turn opened by "SUPERVISOR DORSEY" (low doubt). **How well each is matched.** The strength of the overlap differs and each pairing says so (section N.4): pairing 4 is the same measure (Prop J) with opposite sides on the text; pairing 5 is the same three measures; pairings 1, 2 and 3 are the same subject only (Muni; the city budget and general fund; the inclusionary fee vote and the Prop C package) and say so. A reporter should read 1 to 3 as leads on a subject, not as statements about each other. The money is of three kinds a reader must not confuse: a ballot-measure committee carrying the Mayor's name (1, 2), the official's own committees for supervisor or for another office (3, 4) and old committees for another office (5, years before the supervisor seat). Pairing 5's gifts date from 2012 and 2015.

**What the money search found beyond the pairings.** Money from donor families with a published priority reached: the Mayor's measure committees (Chamber $499K, Airbnb's sponsored committee $500K, POA Issues PAC $10K; Emerson Collective $700K and TWU Local 250-A $50K have no statement found); Dorsey (Realtors $20K, Apartment Association $11K and POA $8K, all into his officeholder committees); Mahmood ($18,000), Sauter ($4,500) and Sherrill ($2,500) from committees named for Govern For California, in gifts of $500 to $5,000; Wong (BOMA's committee $9,999 into a committee formed to support him, a third-party committee) and Chan (BOMA's committee $20,000 into a committee formed to oppose her).

## Keeping the buckets apart

- **Gifts, per committee.** Schedule A rows of the latest version of each filing (the dataset keeps no earlier versions). Totals are per committee; no total joins committees, because they are different recipients. Unitemized receipts carry no name and are not attributed.
- **24-hour reports (form 497 part 1)** are listed apart. A 24-hour row with the same committee, date and amount as a Schedule A row is a repeat and is listed, not counted; one without is "no Schedule A row yet" with its own total. In the Mayor's two measure committees 24-hour-only rows total $1.02M (D, E & F) and $131K (H), outside the Schedule A figures above.
- **Outside spending (form 496)** is by the spending committee. It is not a gift to any committee tied to the official, and it is never added to one. A committee that is tied to the official and also spends (Believe in SF, Lurie for Mayor 2024: $6.73M received in Schedule A and $3.74M of form 496 spending supporting the Mayor in 2024) appears in section 1 as a committee and in section 5 as a spender.
- **Self-funding** is inside the individuals' column: the Mayor's own name accounts for $10.74M of the individual money in his own committee. Section 6 of `funding-map.md` lists it.

## Where the sources fall short

- **Control of measure committees is a name tie.** "Mayor Lurie's Yes on H Committee" and "...Yes on D, E & F Committee" carry the Mayor's name and the Commission's portal shows them as primarily formed measure committees (<https://campaign.sfethics.org/elections/2026-11-03/committees/CA1489257.html>, <https://campaign.sfethics.org/elections/2026-11-03/committees/CA1479782.html>), but neither the datasets nor those pages state that the Mayor controls them (the portal gives a generic definition of candidate-controlled committees). Whether "Believe in SF, Lurie for Mayor 2024" and the committees named for supervisors are controlled by them is likewise not stated.
- **Committees for other offices** (Democratic County Central Committee, College Board) are the official's own, and the Commission's report says county central committee candidate committees are outside the $500 limit; a gift of $2,500 from the POA to Dorsey's officeholder committee for that office is therefore not a breach of the supervisor limit and is shown with its committee name so a reader sees which office it was for.
- **Dates.** Several supervisors' committees are terminated, so recent giving to them is not in the data; Mandelman and Walton show no 2026 committee in either dataset.
- **Names.** Family membership is by FPPC id, then name; a person must read the names as filed. Names that look related but carry no id and no sponsor words (for example a union's "COPE" fund under another name) would be missed.

## How this serves the objectives

- *Everything shown is current and correct*: quotes are re-read from the transcripts word for word at build; statements are re-read from the donors' pages at collection and the run stops if one is gone; money is stamped with the dataset's load date; each pairing says which kind of committee received the money.
- *Let a reporter quickly understand pay-to-play speech*: section 4 of the map gives a reporter a start from the money (the families that gave, the officials that received, and whether the family has a citable statement), and each pairing is a one-screen lead. Nobody was timed; the key result that waits on timing reporters is not met here.

## How to regenerate

`python collect.py` (network: DataSF queries, about two minutes, and the donors' pages) then `python build_map.py` (no network). `python collect.py --money` and `--pages` run either half. `python -m unittest discover -s tests` checks that quotes are verbatim, that every pairing is complete, that 24-hour repeats are not counted and that no text uses a causal word. Standard library only.

## Open questions

**Looked for and not found**
- Any statement that the Mayor or a supervisor controls a named committee: not in the Transactions or Filers datasets (no control field), nor on the Commission's committee pages read. The Commission's Form 410 filings would say; they were not read.
- Priorities in the donor's own words for SEIU 2015, IFPTE Local 21, the Realtors, TWU Local 250-A, Operating Engineers Local 3, Laborers Local 261 and Emerson Collective: the home pages (and, for IFPTE Local 21, its news list) were requested and held no statement on a city measure or policy; position pages (the Realtors', the SFAA slate card, the Labor Council's endorsements) were not read. The Labor Council's endorsement page returned no text to a plain request (it needs a browser) and the Operating Engineers and Laborers sites were not found.
- A link from a transcript stamp to the same moment in the archived video: not in any source read here or in the earlier surveys.
- Supervisors' ballot-measure committees: none found by name; committees that carry no official's name would not appear.
- Individuals' own priorities for the Mayor's largest individual donors beyond the earlier survey's classes: not looked for.

**Claims not verified**
- That "GFC Courage Committee" chapters and "Govern for California Courage Committee" are Govern For California's: read from the names as filed; most of their FPPC ids do not resolve in the Filers dataset (they are state committees), and the organisation's page read says it is paid for by Govern For California, a 501(c)(4), without naming the committees (unverified).
- That "Dignity CA SEIU Local 2015" is SEIU Local 2015's committee, from the name alone (unverified).
- That the POA Issues PAC (filer record status "terminated" in the Filers dataset) made the $10,000 gift of 27 August 2026 to the Mayor's D, E & F committee: the row is in the data with that name and FPPC id 1317554; why a committee marked terminated gave is not explained (unverified).
- That the Commission's August 2026 proposal on candidate-controlled committees has not been adopted since: the source read is a GrowSF page of 20 August 2026 saying it needs eight Board votes and takes effect no earlier than 1 January 2027 (unverified).
- That Prop C, the fee cut and the Housing Trust Fund are one package, as pairing 3 reads them: the transcript's own words at 01:46:56 to 01:47:00 and GrowSF's page say so; the donor (POA) says only to vote Yes on Prop C (my reading).
- That Mahmood is the speaker in pairing 4: two pages read on 2026-10-30 name him as introducer; the captions do not spell his name.

**Questions for people**
- Maintainer: is a gift into a committee formed to support or oppose an official (not the official's own) "money given through committees tied to that official", or only shown apart as here? Pairings use only the official's own and measure committees.
- Maintainer: should a committee that only carries the official's name (the Mayor's measure committees) count as "controlled by" the official in what the site says, pending the Commission's Form 410 records?
- Two or three reporters or watchdogs: verify these five pairings from the files alone, with the clock running; that is the timing the key result waits for.
