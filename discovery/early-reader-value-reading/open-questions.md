## What the maintainer can answer

- **Whether the site may record where visitors came from (the referring page) on card requests, without recording who they are, and whether a daily visit count may be kept.** Not answered. Signs 1, 4, 7, 8 and 9 need it. mySociety notes civic technologists generally collect as little as possible about users.

The maintainer said "I have none to ask"; whether they would ask strangers is not said (my reading: not ruled out, not offered).
- Whether the feedback entry may carry optional fields (outlet, how to reply, kind) so reporters can be told apart and written back to. The maintainer asked for feedback to go "in an admin queue" and not "directly send email"; whether a reply goes out of the queue and by what means is not said.
