# First reading sheet: counting rules, weekly sheet and questions for early reporter readers

**Kind of document: a protocol with blank tables.** It is what one person fills in over the first weeks of use. Nothing below has been filled: no reading was taken for this survey. The tables are left empty on purpose so a later reading is not mistaken for a result.

## Who counts as an early reporter reader

A person who writes or edits for a news outlet or independent news project covering San Francisco and who has said so: in a feedback entry, in a conversation, or by a story that names the site. Record the name and outlet in a private list; do not publish it. Anyone else is a reader, not a reporter, in every table.

The maintainer's answer: "No reporter has used it and I have none to ask." The list therefore starts empty and cannot be filled by inviting named people; it fills only as reporters find the site and identify themselves. In the weekly sheet, "Reporters invited" is zero until that changes, and a week with no reporters is a recorded week, not a missing one.

## Questions for each early reader (ten minutes, same order, answers in their words)

Use them with any reporter who gets in touch and is willing to talk, and as optional fields on a feedback entry where a short form is wanted. No reporter is lined up to be asked.

Write what they say in quotation marks as they said it; do not paraphrase.

1. What did you do with the site the first time? (Looked, copied a quote, checked a source, wrote it into a draft, nothing.)
2. Did you open the transcript, the recording, the donor page or a filing? Which, and did it match?
3. Would you put a card in a story? If not, what stops you?
4. What did you look for that was not there?
5. What would make you come back next week without being asked?
6. Is there anything on a card you think is wrong or could be read as claiming more than it shows?
7. How else would you have found this information, and how long would that have taken?

## What has to exist before the sheet can be filled

Signs 2, 5 and 6 are filled from the feedback route the maintainer wants on the cards, whose entries sit in an admin queue and are not sent as email. Each entry needs, to be countable, the card it concerns, a kind (correction, request, other) and a date; an optional outlet field and an optional way to reply let signs 3 and 11 be read and let a reporter be asked the questions above. Sign 1 can also use a record of the referrer on card requests, which the maintainer has not yet answered on. Signs 3, 8 and 9 need more (a counter on the button and on outbound links). Until then only 7, as raw requests per path, comes from the site; sign 1 comes from searching the web and news. Nothing was built for this survey.
